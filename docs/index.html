About

Three generations. No job too big or too small.

Matt James Rubbish Removals is a local family business, now in its third generation with over 30 years of experience, based in Melbourne’s northern suburbs.

We remove anything from garden waste and general rubbish to office furniture, electrical appliances and refurbishment waste, from anywhere on your property. Unlike a skip, you pay for the amount of rubbish we clear, so there’s no guessing the container size. We recycle what we can and keep a large share of every load out of landfill.
